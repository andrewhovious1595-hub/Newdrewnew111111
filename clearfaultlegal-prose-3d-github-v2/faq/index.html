Pro Se Forms & Litigation Suite FAQ

Fast answers about our verified court forms database, Word/PDF pleading exports, probate & bankruptcy packets, browser privacy, and legal boundaries.

What court forms are in the Clear Fault Legal database?

Our Master Court Forms Database indexes verified official U.S. District Court Pro Se civil forms (Pro Se 1–14, AO 240, AO 88A/88B), complete Federal Bankruptcy forms (B 101 through B 122A-1 for Chapter 7 & 13), and California Judicial Council Civil, Probate (DE-111, DE-150, GC-310), Small Claims (SC-100–SC-120), Discovery (DISC-001–DISC-020), Fee Waiver (FW-001/FW-003), and Appeal forms—each linked directly to its official .gov judiciary portal.

Can I draft a Civil Complaint, Answer, Motion, or Declaration right here?

Yes. Launch the Pro Se Pleading & Motion Drafter to build properly captioned filings with numbered factual paragraphs, exhibit citations, relief requested, and penalty-of-perjury verification blocks—exportable directly to Word (.doc), Print/PDF, TXT, and JSON.

What if I just want to send a settlement demand letter before suing?

Open the Demand Package Assembler. Enter your facts, liability summary, itemized special damages (bills, repairs, lost wages, unpaid invoices), and enclosure list to generate a formal, printable settlement demand package with automatic totals and response deadlines.

Can I use this for Probate or Bankruptcy without hiring a lawyer first?

Yes. Use our Probate and Bankruptcy filing tracks to pull the exact official forms required (such as DE-111/DE-150 for Probate or B 101–B 122A-1 for Chapter 7/13), export a custom filing packet checklist, draft companion asset/creditor worksheets, and index all financial records locally.

Are my confidential case files or financial records uploaded to a server?

Never. Every single tool runs 100% locally inside your web browser. Your uploaded scene photos, local PDFs dropped into the Bates Indexer, creditor schedules, and draft complaints never leave your device until you export them.

Is Clear Fault Legal a law firm or legal advice?

No. Clear Fault Legal provides self-service drafting tools, visual exhibit builders, and verified links to official judiciary form portals. No tool on this website provides legal advice or replaces consulting a licensed attorney regarding statutes of limitations, local court rules, or legal strategy.
